Professional Mattress Cleaning Services in Melbourne

You spend roughly a third of your life in bed — but when was the last time your mattress had a proper deep clean? Over months and years, dead skin cells, sweat, body oils, dust mites, bacteria, and mould spores accumulate deep within your mattress. This invisible build-up can trigger allergies, asthma, skin irritation, and disrupt your sleep quality. Regular vacuuming barely scratches the surface.

At BABA Cleaning, our professional mattress cleaning Melbourne service uses advanced steam cleaning equipment to reach deep into the mattress core, killing bacteria and dust mites on contact. Combined with eco-friendly, fully organic cleaning products, we eliminate stains, odours, and allergens — leaving you with a hygienically clean mattress that promotes better sleep and better health.

Our Mattress Cleaning Process

Every mattress receives a thorough, multi-step treatment designed to sanitise from surface to core:

  • Mattress inspection — We assess the mattress type, fabric, stain severity, and areas of concern before starting
  • Dry vacuuming — High-powered vacuuming extracts surface dust, dead skin cells, and loose debris from the entire mattress
  • Stain pre-treatment — Urine, sweat, blood, food, and drink stains are individually treated with safe, organic solutions
  • Steam sanitisation — High-temperature steam penetrates deep into the mattress to kill dust mites, bacteria, mould, and allergens
  • Odour neutralisation — Embedded smells from sweat, urine, and mildew are neutralised at the source — not just masked
  • Fully organic — We use no harsh chemicals. Our products are safe for you, your children, and your pets

Mattress Cleaning FAQ

How often should I have my mattress professionally cleaned?

We recommend every 6–12 months. If you suffer from allergies, asthma, or have young children, every 6 months is ideal. Regular cleaning keeps dust mite populations under control and extends your mattress life.

Can you remove urine stains and smell from a mattress?

Yes. Urine stains are one of the most common mattress issues we tackle. Our enzymatic pre-treatment breaks down uric acid crystals, and the steam extraction removes both the stain and the odour from deep within the mattress.

Is your mattress cleaning safe for babies and children?

Absolutely. We use fully organic, non-toxic cleaning products with no harsh chemicals. Our steam cleaning method is chemical-free — the high-temperature steam does the heavy lifting. It's completely safe for babies, children, and pets.

How long does mattress cleaning take? When can I sleep on it?

Cleaning takes approximately 30–45 minutes per mattress. Drying time is typically 2–4 hours depending on ventilation. We recommend booking a morning appointment so your mattress is ready by bedtime.

Do you clean all mattress types?

Yes — we clean innerspring, memory foam, latex, pillow-top, hybrid, and futon mattresses. All sizes from single to king. We adjust our technique based on the mattress construction to ensure safe, effective cleaning.
